Stanford Lucy
============
Original model: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory
Repository: https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/#lucy
Downloaded mesh: three.js r180, examples/models/ply/binary/Lucy100k.ply
URL: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r180/examples/models/ply/binary/Lucy100k.ply
Retrieved: 2026-11-08
Source PLY SHA-256: 837f769e67155dc4a6e9a90683a61d6f6b4571a2a97d79586831011f450e66e2

This reduced mesh has 50002 vertices and 100000 triangles.
Converted to OBJ without changing topology. Positions were uniformly scaled
by 1/1596.901123046875 and translated to unit height, Y-up, base Y=0,
center X=Z=0. Area-weighted vertex normals were generated from outward-wound
triangles. Every edge has exactly two incident faces.
